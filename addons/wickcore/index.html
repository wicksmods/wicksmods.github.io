WickCore

The floor everything else stands on.

version 0.14.0/wickcorefree, open source
Get it on CurseForge → Source on GitHub Discord

What it does

  • Brand chrome, profiles and themes
  • One options panel for the suite
  • Client dialect shim and restriction guard
  • Minimap launcher and version broadcast
  • Optional settings store in macros

Latest changes

0.14.0 2026-11-05

Changed

  • Cartridge keeps the minimap square, in a window of its own, whatever shape the other looks use. Products read it from the look.
  • Cartridge draws all its text in Press Start 2P (OFL), console letters eight dots square, in place of Micro 5. Every size is worked out in screen pixels at the interface scale you play at, so every dot is one, two or three whole pixels: small labels at one, most text at two, big titles at three. The drop shadow is one dot of the text it is under.
  • Text in a tight slot, made for the game's narrow fonts (a unit frame's name, an info bar slot, a button's keybind), stays at one pixel a dot in Cartridge, so it fits.
  • The Wick's Mods options page sizes its theme swatches, style buttons and color pickers to their names, so a wide face no longer runs them into each other. In the other looks nothing moves.
  • A look can ask a full interface to put its face on the game's own fonts (gameFonts). Cartridge does.
  • Cartridge draws icons in dots: each icon becomes a grid twelve dots square, every dot one color sampled live from the game's own icon, so nothing of the game's art is shipped. Products hand their icons to it.
  • Cartridge has a capsule for nameplates, as a 16-bit action game's life bar: rounded ends in dots, the bevel following the curve, a capsule track inside. A frame asks for it with Chrome:SetPanelShape.
  • The look buttons say that names and damage numbers over the world change at your next login, as the game reads their font only then.

0.13.0 2026-11-05

Added

  • Cartridge, a 16-bit look drawn as the consoles drew, every dot two screen pixels: chunky windows with a black dot round them, the corner dots knocked out and a three-dot bevel inside, lit from the top by a gradient; square icons and minimap; flat bars with a lit top edge; and blocky pixel text (Micro 5, OFL) drawn without smoothing, with a two-pixel drop shadow. Every size goes on a ladder of five that all draw as clean blocks, so small labels stay readable and titles stay chunky, and frames grow to fit their text where they have room. Gold on console blue. Every Wick addon follows it, like any other look.

0.12.1 2026-11-05

Changed

  • The client report line (flavor, build, interface, dialect and the rest) is gone from the foot of the Wick's Mods options page. It is still printed by /wickcore.
