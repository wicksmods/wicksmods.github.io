Wick's Gear

The best gear upgrade for every slot while leveling, scored for your class against what you are wearing, from dungeon drops and crafted gear, with where to get it. Browse dungeon loot by boss, crafted gear, quest rewards and sets, and try pieces on a paperdoll to see what they would do to your stats, green up and red down.

version 0.9.11/wgearfree, open source
Get it on CurseForge → Source on GitHub Discord

What it does

  • Your best in slot pick for every slot, from dungeon drops and crafted gear
  • Dungeon loot by boss, crafted gear, quest rewards and sets
  • Set bonuses you have earned and what the next one needs
  • Try pieces on to see your stats change, green up and red down

Needs WickCore, the shared base every Wick addon draws from. CurseForge installs it alongside.

Latest changes

0.9.11 2026-11-21

Added

  • Upgrades scores the rare quest rewards of the zones as well, and names the zone. It now draws on dungeon drops, crafted gear and quest rewards.

Fixed

  • A quest reward only the other faction's quest gives is no longer listed or suggested. A Horde character saw Ironforge's rewards, and an Alliance one Orgrimmar's.
  • A quest reward made for another class is not suggested as an upgrade, and the Equippable switch hides it in Browse.

0.9.10 2026-11-21

Added

  • Upgrades scores crafted gear as well as dungeon drops, and names the profession and the skill the recipe needs. A character below level 9 had an empty list, because no dungeon drop can be worn before then.

Fixed

  • An empty Upgrades list no longer says the item data has not arrived.
  • The tooltip of a crafted piece said it drops in a dungeon.

0.9.9 2026-11-15

Added

  • Dungeon gear goes from 430 pieces to 1,603, across the release's 27 dungeons. The lists were collected during the beta, which had fewer dungeons and thinner loot tables than the game shipped with.
  • Five dungeons that were missing: City of Dalaran, The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison and Blackmaw Hold.
  • The Hall of Thanes and the Ruins of Lordaeron list what their bosses drop. Before, they had quest rewards and nothing else.
  • The level 50 to 60 dungeons are filled in. Blackrock Depths has 169 pieces where it had 28, Dire Maul 161 where it had 2, Blackrock Spire 154 where it had 33, Stratholme 146, Scholomance 108, and the Temple of Atal'Hakkar 54 where it had 1.
  • Quest rewards from the release's new zones, Riverglades, Shen'dralas and Mount Hyjal, and from zones the beta had nothing recorded for: Ironforge, Wetlands, Desolace, Un'Goro Crater, Alterac Mountains, the Hinterlands and the Blasted Lands. 358 rare and better rewards across 24 zones, where there were 179 across 15.
  • The recipes the release added: 69 more crafted pieces across Blacksmithing, Leatherworking and Tailoring, 1,356 in all.
  • Four sets new to the release: Defias Enforcer's Garb, Krol'dok Battlegear, Rotmender's Raiment and Violet Sorcerer's Vestments.

Fixed

  • The character in the wardrobe flashed, or never finished drawing, while the lists were loading. The model was rebuilt every time item data arrived, about three times a second, and with this many items that went on for a minute. It is rebuilt only when what it wears changes, and once when the window opens.

Changed

  • Level brackets are the release's. Sixteen of them had moved since the beta: the Deadmines is 18 to 23 now, Dire Maul 58 to 60.
  • Every dungeon stat line was read again from the live game's tooltips. A third of the pieces already listed had been retuned since the beta.
  • A piece is listed under a dungeon only when it names a boss, or the dungeon itself, as where it comes from. World drops that happened to be looted there are left out.
  • Stranglethorn Vale is one zone in this game and is named as one.

Removed

  • Eight pieces the release no longer ties to a dungeon: Face Smasher, Haunting Blade, Phantom Armor and Abomination Skin Leggings from Shadowfang Keep, "Mage-Eye" Blunderbuss from Razorfen Kraul, Pendant of Myzrael and Scorn's Icy Choker from Scarlet Monastery, and Idol of the Dream from the Temple of Atal'Hakkar.
  • Five rewards from the Barrens that are no longer listed as rare.
  • Seven crafted pieces the release has no recipe for: Golden Scale Gauntlets, Turtle Scale Gloves, Phoenix Bindings, Invoker's Cord, Invoker's Mantle, Leather-Reinforced Runecloth Bag and SAF-T Teleport.

Not in yet

  • Shaper's Terrace. Nobody has recorded what it drops, and a guess is worse than a gap.
  • City of Dalaran has its quest rewards only, and Krol'dok Stronghold's drops are not tied to a boss yet.
  • Raids. None is open yet.

The full changelog is in the repository.
