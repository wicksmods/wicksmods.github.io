Wick's Map

Scroll the world map from the world down to the ground, and pin your own places.

version 0.1.0/wmapfree, open source
Get it on CurseForge → Source on GitHub Discord

What it does

  • A glide between map levels
  • Terrain past the painted map
  • Your own pins, and an arrow

Needs WickCore, the shared base every Wick addon draws from. CurseForge installs it alongside.

Latest changes

0.1.0 2026-10-23

The first release.

Added

  • Scrolling the world map moves between levels: in over a continent opens it, in over a zone opens that, out goes back up. Where a map zooms, the game's own zoom runs to its end first.
  • Each zoom step glides instead of jumping, with one spot held still, and a new level opens zoomed so that spot stays there. Can be turned off.
  • With the cursor over the part of the map you are in, the zoom closes in on you and brings you toward the middle; anywhere else, or with Shift held, it zooms where the cursor is. Can be turned off.
  • A short zoom between levels, drawn from the game's own map art, with the smaller map coming in from its place on the larger one. Can be turned off.
  • Zoomed in on a zone, the minimap's terrain tiles fade in under the map's pins, on the Eastern Kingdoms, Kalimdor and Outland. At the game's closest zoom it is all terrain, and the wheel goes on past that step, about as close as the minimap's closest zoom; scrolling on once there leaves the view where it is. Can be turned off.
  • Pins of your own: right-click the world map to add one with a name, a note and a raid marker icon. Kept between sessions on every character, shown on their zone and smaller on the continent. Hover to read one, left-click to head there, right-click to change or delete it. Shift+right-click still goes up a level.
  • Left-click a pin to head for it: it glows, and a small window with an arrow and the distance in yards points the way, clearing on arrival. Left-click again to stop; deleting the pin stops it too.
  • The same menu sets a waypoint anywhere, with the same arrow. On Forever it is the game's own waypoint, also shown on the world map and the minimap; on TBC a marker of ours on the map. One place to head for at a time.
  • From chat you can pin the spot you are standing on, list your pins, or set a waypoint by its coordinates. The README lists the commands.

The full changelog is in the repository.
